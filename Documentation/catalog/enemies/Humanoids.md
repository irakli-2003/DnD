
## Humanoids

### Humans

#### Bandit
    • Category: Humanoid / Human
    • Threat Level: Low
    • Size: Medium
    • HP: 20
    • Armor: Leather scraps or none
    • Description: A rough-looking human in travel-stained clothes, with a weapon-ready stance and a suspicious glare.
    • Abilities: Ambush, dirty fighting, coordinated robbery
    • Damage: 1d6 slashing
    • Weaknesses: Discipline, fear, superior range
    • Resistances: Minor intimidation, simple opportunism
    • Habitat: Roads, forests, abandoned camps
    • Loot: Coin pouch, crude blade, worn gear; XP: 5

## Notes on Use
    • These entries are intended as common-world encounters rather than legendary threats.
    • XP values are intentionally modest and should fit low-to-mid level adventuring.
    • You can add shops, factions, or regional variants later if you want to expand the catalog.
