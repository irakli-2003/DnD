# Mobs Catalog

This catalog groups ordinary creatures and people into presentable markdown sections. Each entry uses the same compact stats window style as the monster catalog, but the subjects here are beasts and humanoids rather than monsters.

## Beasts

### Bear
    • Category: Beast
    • Threat Level: Medium
    • Size: Large
    • HP: 45
    • Armor: Thick fur and hide
    • Description: A heavy, broad-shouldered forest bear with a large muzzle, dark eyes, and powerful forelimbs.
    • Abilities: Maul, charge, intimidate through size
    • Damage: 2d8 slashing
    • Weaknesses: Fire, nets, strong terrain control
    • Resistances: Cold weather, blunt force resilience
    • Habitat: Forests, hills, mountain trails
    • Loot: Fur, claws, meat; XP: 12

### Wild Dog
    • Category: Beast
    • Threat Level: Low
    • Size: Small-Medium
    • HP: 18
    • Armor: Short fur
    • Description: A lean, wary dog with matted fur, sharp teeth, and a scavenger’s alert posture.
    • Abilities: Pack bite, chase, harass targets
    • Damage: 1d6 piercing
    • Weaknesses: Fire, loud noise, isolation from pack
    • Resistances: Minor pain tolerance, nimble movement
    • Habitat: Roadsides, camps, forests
    • Loot: Hide, teeth, meat; XP: 5

### Wolf
    • Category: Beast
    • Threat Level: Low-Medium
    • Size: Medium
    • HP: 22
    • Armor: Fur and lean muscle
    • Description: A gray or black wolf with a narrow snout, upright ears, and a coiled, predatory stance.
    • Abilities: Pack tactics, pounce, scent tracking
    • Damage: 1d8 piercing
    • Weaknesses: Fire, bright light, stagger effects
    • Resistances: Cold, forest camouflage
    • Habitat: Forests, snowy hills, hunting grounds
    • Loot: Fur, fangs, pelt; XP: 8

### Panther
    • Category: Beast
    • Threat Level: Medium
    • Size: Medium
    • HP: 28
    • Armor: Sleek fur
    • Description: A black-coated jungle cat with smooth muscles, bright eyes, and silent, padded paws.
    • Abilities: Ambush pounce, stealth approach, claw rake
    • Damage: 1d8 slashing
    • Weaknesses: Fire, traps, loud disturbance
    • Resistances: Stealth, quick evasive movement
    • Habitat: Jungles, dense forests, rocky thickets
    • Loot: Fur, claws, teeth; XP: 8


## Notes on Use
    • These entries are intended as common-world encounters rather than legendary threats.
    • XP values are intentionally modest and should fit low-to-mid level adventuring.
    • You can add shops, factions, or regional variants later if you want to expand the catalog.
